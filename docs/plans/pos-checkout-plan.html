Flow (like Ease)

  • The customer strip defaults to Walk-in #N; tap it to search for a guest
  • Tapping a tile adds a row (a service adds one unit; products and nights merge into a quantity)
  • Tap a row to expand its details: therapist (eligible staff only), time, discount and price override
  • "Attach booking" lists the guest's unpaid stays and appointments; nothing is added automatically
  • Entry via ?booking= or ?bill=; ambiguous URLs are refused
  • "Clear bill" asks for confirmation

Build

  • CheckoutGrid, BillPanel (reused in BillSheet), FloatingBill, CustomerStrip
  • Convex bills (status draft, held, paid, void) and billLines
  • billAudit rows for price overrides (who, why, old and new price)
Done when: staff can build a bill for a walk-in or a guest, give each massage its own therapist, override a price with a note, and hold the bill, then resume it on another device.

Backend

  • A single settleBill mutation that takes an idempotency key per payment attempt
  • Assigns a per-tenant receipt number in the same transaction; numbers are never reused, even after a void
  • Marks the attached bookings and appointments paid
  • Deducts the deposit (Stripe online payment or a manual deposit)
  • VAT mode setting: included, added or exempt; optional card surcharge
  • Void with reason; voided receipts stay on record

Frontend

  • Payment types: cash, PromptPay QR (staff confirms), card, transfer; split up to 3 methods
  • Cash panel with quick amounts and change due
  • Mutation lock, so a double tap doesn't charge twice
  • After paying, redirect to /admin/receipt/[billId]
  • Thermal print styles for 58mm and 80mm paper
Done when: a split cash plus QR payment settles once even if retried, the receipt number has no gaps, and void keeps the record.
