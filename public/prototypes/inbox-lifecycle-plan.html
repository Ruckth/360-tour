    What changes for the responder?

    1
    Send is the main action

    No extra Resolve click after a successful answer.

    2
    Keep open only when work remains

    Use “Add follow-up” or “Keep open.” Track the task explicitly, rather than guessing from reply text.

    3
    Keep the transcript on screen

    Show “Done automatically” with Undo. Let the responder choose the next conversation.

    4
    Let AI handle the next issue

    Staff takeover ends when the issue is done, unless “Keep with staff” is selected. Reopen and No reply needed stay in the secondary menu.

    How we build it

    01 · One shared lifecycle

    The website and every messaging channel use the same backend rules. Store which guest turn was answered, the send outcome, staff tasks, and who handles the chat.

    02 · Resolve from confirmed outcomes

    AI returns Answered, Waiting for guest, or Needs staff. Handoffs and failed operations override “Answered.” A message being written to the transcript alone is not proof it was sent.

    03 · Protect newer questions

    Every reply is linked to the guest turn it covers. Finishing an older reply must never close a newer question. Apply completion and status updates together; retries and duplicate webhooks are idempotent.

    04 · Update queues and controls

    Needs you replaces Waiting. In progress replaces Open. Done stays. A closed channel reply window shows a restriction and leaves unresolved work visible.

    05 · Clean up existing chats carefully

    Preview a backfill first. Move clearly answered chats with no outstanding work to Done. Leave ambiguous chats for review; keep their history.

    06 · Verify before rollout

    Check successful answers, handoffs, failures, staff tasks, new messages during sending, duplicate events, and reopening across every channel.

    Confirmed: immediate Done + automatic reopening.
